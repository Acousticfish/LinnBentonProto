What to expect

Just walk in

No appointment, no referral, no paperwork. Open meetings welcome anyone; closed meetings are for people who think they may have a drug problem. Both are listed on the schedule.

You don't have to speak

Sit in the back and listen for an hour if that's all you can do. People will say hello, and that's about the extent of what's asked of you.

What's said here stays here

Anonymity is the spiritual foundation of our traditions. First names only, and nothing leaves the room.

The area

From the valley floor to the coast range

The Linn Benton Area carries the message in Albany, Corvallis, Lebanon, Sweet Home and Philomath — plus the Canal Creek Campout each summer, where the fellowship spends a weekend in the trees.

For the public

Working with professionals

The Public Relations Subcommittee helps groups carry the NA message: meeting schedules, this website, the phone line, and presentations for prospective members and the professionals who encounter them.

We share our message with the public at large, with prospective members, and with professionals in courts, treatment centers, hospitals and schools. If your organization works with people who use drugs, we will bring a presentation and a stack of schedules at no cost.

Request a presentation

What NA is

A nonprofit fellowship of recovering addicts who meet regularly to help each other stay clean. Membership is free and open to any drug addict, regardless of the drug.

What NA is not

Not a treatment program, not affiliated with any other organization, and we take no position on outside issues. We don't provide detox, housing, counseling or legal services.

Court cards & verification

Individual groups decide whether to sign attendance slips. Ask the group's secretary before the meeting starts, not during.
